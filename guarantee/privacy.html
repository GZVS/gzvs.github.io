Privacy Policy

Guarantee

Effective 28 October 2026

In short: Guarantee does not collect, receive or share any personal data. Your items, receipts and letters stay on your iPhone and, if you use iCloud, in your own private iCloud storage, which I cannot access. There are no accounts, no ads, no analytics and no tracking.

Who I am

Guarantee is made by Georgios Zacharidis (GZVS), Thessaloniki, Greece. For any privacy question, write to gzvsDev@icloud.com.

Your items and receipts

  • The items you add, with their receipt photos, PDFs, label photos and notes, are stored on your iPhone.
  • If you're signed in to iCloud, they are also synced through your private iCloud database, so they appear on your other devices. Apple stores this under your Apple Account and the Apple Privacy Policy; the developer has no access to it.
  • Receipts are kept as the picture or PDF you add. Nothing is uploaded to me or to any server of mine.

Camera and photos

  • The camera is used only when you choose to scan a receipt.
  • When you pick an existing photo, Apple's photo picker shows Guarantee only the photo you choose, not the rest of your library.

Your name and address

If you add your name, address, email or phone in Settings, they are kept on your iPhone and printed only on the claim letters you create.

Claim letters and email

When you email a claim, Guarantee opens Apple's Mail with the letter and your receipt attached. You review and send it yourself, from your own email account, directly to the shop. Sharing a letter as a PDF hands it to the app you choose, and that app's privacy policy applies from then on.

Reminders

Expiry reminders are local notifications scheduled on your iPhone. No push service of mine is involved.

What I don't do

  • No accounts or sign-in.
  • No analytics, crash reporting SDKs, advertising or tracking of any kind.
  • No cookies, no servers of my own and no third-party SDKs.
  • No selling or sharing of data. There is no data to sell or share.

Children

Guarantee is suitable for all ages and collects no personal data from anyone, including children.

Your rights and deleting your data

Because I hold no personal data about you, there is nothing for me to access, correct or delete. You control everything: delete an item in the app and it is removed from your iPhone and your iCloud. Uninstalling the app removes what it stored on your iPhone; its iCloud data can be removed in your iPhone's iCloud storage settings. If you have a question or a request under the GDPR or any other privacy law, email gzvsDev@icloud.com.

Changes

If this policy changes, the new version will be posted on this page with a new effective date.
