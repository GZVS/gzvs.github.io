Privacy Policy

Padel Hunter

Effective 2 November 2026

In short: Padel Hunter does not collect, sell or share your personal data. There are no ads, no analytics and no tracking. The game has no accounts and no servers of its own.

Who I am

Padel Hunter is made by Georgios Zacharidis (GZVS), Thessaloniki, Greece. For any privacy question, write to gzvsDev@icloud.com.

What stays on your device

Your settings, best score, coins, unlocked rackets and cosmetics, mission progress and Daily Challenge streak are stored on your iPhone.

iCloud

If you're signed in to iCloud, the same progress is synced between your own devices through Apple's iCloud key-value storage. It's stored in your iCloud account, which I can't access. Turning iCloud off for the app keeps everything on the device only.

Game Center

Leaderboards and achievements use Apple Game Center. If you sign in to Game Center, your scores and achievements are sent to Apple and shown with your Game Center nickname on leaderboards, including to your Game Center friends. If you allow it, the game reads your Game Center friends list only to show the Friends leaderboard. This is handled by Apple under the Apple Privacy Policy. You can play without signing in.

In-app purchases

Coin packs and the Starter Pack are bought through Apple's App Store. Apple processes the payment; I never see your name, Apple Account or payment details. The game only receives confirmation that a purchase happened, so it can deliver your coins.

Sharing your score

"Challenge a friend" opens the standard iOS share sheet with a short text containing your score. Nothing is sent unless you choose where to share it, and the app you choose applies its own privacy policy.

What I don't do

  • No accounts or sign-in of my own.
  • No analytics, crash reporting SDKs, advertising or tracking of any kind.
  • No cookies, no servers of my own and no third-party SDKs.
  • No selling or sharing of data. There is no data to sell or share.

Children

Padel Hunter collects no personal information from anyone, including children.

Your rights and deleting your data

Because I hold no personal data about you, there is nothing for me to access, correct or delete. Deleting the app removes your progress from your iPhone; a copy synced through iCloud stays in your private iCloud storage. If you have a question or a request under the GDPR or any other privacy law, email gzvsDev@icloud.com.

Changes

If this policy changes, the new version will be posted on this page with a new effective date.
