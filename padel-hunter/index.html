A one-tap laser padel arcade. Rally against the glass and climb the world leaderboards.

Need help? Email me and I'll get back to you, usually within two working days. Please include your iPhone model and iOS version if something isn't working.

Questions

How do I play?

A laser sweeps across the glass wall. Tap to serve the ball at whatever it points to. When the ball comes back, tap again at the right moment to return it and keep the rally going. A perfectly timed return flies straight to the laser dot. A short tutorial runs the first time you open the game; replay it any time from Settings › Replay Tutorial.

I bought coins but they didn't arrive

Close and reopen the game while you're online: purchases that didn't finish are delivered when the game starts. If they still don't appear, email me with the date of the purchase (it's in Apple's receipt email).

I reinstalled the game or got a new iPhone

If iCloud is on, your coins, unlocked rackets and progress come back by themselves. To get the Starter Pack back, open Settings › Restore Purchases. Apple can't restore coin packs, because they're used up when bought, but coins that synced through iCloud stay.

How do I unlock rackets?

Each racket shows its goal in the shop, such as rounds played, a best score, a long rally, missions or Daily Challenges. You can also unlock any of them with coins. Every racket has a strength and a weakness, so none of them is simply better than the others and the leaderboards stay fair.

What are the monthly sets?

A new set of racket colors, balls, lasers and a court arrives in the shop at the start of a month. Sets stay in the shop once they're out.

My score isn't on the leaderboard

Leaderboards use Apple Game Center. Sign in from Ranks in the game or in the iPhone's Settings › Game Center. Scores from rounds played offline are sent the next time you play online.

Does the game have ads?

No, never. The game is free; coin packs and the one-time Starter Pack are optional.

Refunds

Purchases are handled by Apple. You can ask for a refund at reportaproblem.apple.com.

What happens to my data?

Your progress stays on your iPhone and in your own iCloud. Padel Hunter has no accounts, no ads and no tracking. Read the privacy policy.
